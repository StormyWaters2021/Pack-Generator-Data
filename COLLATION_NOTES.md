# Collation Notes — Lantern Legacy and X-Men '97

These V1 configs are empirical models built from complete sealed brick/case openings and the current OCTGN set data.
Published V1 configs should be treated as immutable once share codes are issued; refine uncertain rates in V2.

## Lantern Legacy V1

Structural rules encoded with high confidence:
- 2 bricks per case; 12 boosters per brick.
- Every booster has 4 character slots plus 1 Lantern special slot.
- The best fully enumerated brick supports a base mix of 10 boosters at 2 Common / 1 Uncommon / 1 Rare and 2 boosters at 1 Common / 2 Uncommon / 1 Rare.
- Exactly 3 SR-family hits per brick, each replacing a Rare slot.
- Exactly 1 standard Chase per brick, replacing a Rare slot.
- Prime overlays the matching printed-rarity slot: Rare Prime -> Rare; SR Prime -> SR-family hit.
- Exactly 1 Legacy Card topper per brick. This is independently confirmed by multiple sealed-brick openings and a detailed Action Figure Insider brick review.
- Every booster receives one additional card drawn from Legacy Card / Alternate Card (Team-Up) / One Shot, for 12 booster inserts per brick in addition to the topper.

Empirical V1 rates:
- Prime count: 0 or 1 per brick at equal weight, based on 4 Prime-bearing bricks in 8 complete brick observations.
- Prime printed rarity: Rare:SR is weighted 5:1. All four directly observed Lantern Prime pulls were Rare Primes; the nonzero SR weight keeps the printed SR Primes reachable while treating them as substantially scarcer.
- Ring count: 3/4/5 per brick with weights 1/1/2, matching observed complete-brick ring totals in the current sample.

Lantern special pools:
- Rings: s001-s010.
- Non-ring fifth-slot specials: s011-s050, covering the generic special objects/equipment plus their colored construct versions.
- The generic Sound Wave and Chain are directly observed sealed-booster pulls, so they are intentionally included.

Standard booster Chase pool is 051-060. 099-101 are excluded from standard sealed Chase collation.

Observed Lantern openings also show curated/theme boosters where several pulls share a Corps/color. V1 models the proven marginal slot distribution but does not force a correlated theme-booster bundle; add that as a later engine/config version if we reconstruct the bundle templates and frequency precisely.

## X-Men '97 V1

Structural rules encoded with high confidence:
- 2 bricks per case; 12 boosters per brick; 5 figures per booster.
- Base brick uses 8 boosters at 3C/1U/1R and 4 boosters at 2C/2U/1R.
- Exactly 1 Common-slot SR-family hit per brick.
- An additional 2 or 3 SR-family hits replace Rare slots, yielding 3 or 4 SR-family hits total.
- Exactly 1 standard Chase per brick, replacing a Rare slot.
- Rare Prime Bishop overlays Rare; SR Primes Cable/Rogue/Emma Frost overlay SR-family hits.
- Wolverine 011b is a separate secret Chase replacing a Common slot and does not replace the standard Chase.
- Exactly 1 Alternate Card topper per brick.
- Booster One-Shot pool is OS001 Magneto Was Right and OS004 Sinister Science only.
- OS002 Omega Level Threat Detected and OS003 Practiced Teamwork are kit products and are excluded from booster collation.
- TM001-TM004 terrain markers are excluded from boosters; observed source material places terrain in the Play-at-Home kit rather than sealed boosters.

Empirical V1 rates:
- Rare-slot SR count: 2 with weight 1, 3 with weight 2. Combined with the guaranteed Common-slot SR, this reproduces the observed 3-vs-4 SR-family split (2/6 vs 4/6 complete bricks).
- Prime count: 0 or 1 at equal weight, matching the direct complete-brick sample (3 Prime-bearing bricks in 6). A secondary report mentions a two-Prime brick, but V1 does not encode that until a complete source is reconstructed; G3 also cannot safely place two Prime overlays on distinct slots in every roll.
- Prime printed rarity: Rare:SR weighted 1:2, matching the three directly reconstructed Prime-bearing bricks (Bishop Rare Prime once; SR Prime twice).
- Secret Wolverine 011b: 1-in-6 brick probability from the current complete-brick sample.

Standard booster Chase pool is 043-049. 099-101 are excluded from standard sealed Chase collation.
